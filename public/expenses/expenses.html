Rewards Eligibility

About

Claim $5 reward for using more cashless transactions.

Our bank wants to encourage customers to use more cashless payments instead of withdrawing and using cash. To support this, we have introduced a Cashless Rewards Program. This program rewards customers who rely more on digital transactions like card payments, mobile payments, or online transfers.

Each month, we will check the percentage of cash withdrawals compared to all transactions made. If the amount of cash withdrawn is below a certain percentage, the customer will be eligible for the reward. This way, the more you use digital payments, the higher your chances of receiving a reward.

Rewards Eligibility Criteria

  • Users must ensure that their total cash withdrawals account for less than 15% of their total monthly account transactions.
  • Users should perform at least 2 transactions (e.g., card payments, mobile payments, or online transfers, cash redrawal) within the same month.
  • Users must comply with all terms and conditions of the bank, including adhering to any anti-fraud and responsible usage policies.
