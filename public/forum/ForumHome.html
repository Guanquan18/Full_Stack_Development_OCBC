OCBC Discussion Forum

Welcome to OCBC's Community Forum which is your space to communicate and collaborate with other members of OCBC.

This platform also serves as a reliable communication channel during the rare instances of system downtime. If our helpline services are temporarily unavailable, you can count on the forum to stay informed, share updates, and find solutions in collaboration with other users.

Key Features:

  • Engage in discussions - Share your thoughts on a wide range of topics from personal finance to feedback about our services.
  • Collaborate and connect - Build a network with like-minded people and learn from each other's experiences.
  • Stay updated - Access the latest updates and community-driven solutions during critical service disruptions.
