Scam Awareness Quiz

The scam awareness quiz in the OCBC app is designed to educate elderly users and individuals with limited financial literacy about common scams and fraudulent activities. This interactive feature aims to enhance their awareness of the tactics scammers use, such as phishing emails, fake calls, and deceptive online offers.

By simulating real-world scenarios, the quiz helps users identify red flags and equips them with practical tips to safeguard their personal information and finances.

The ultimate goal is to empower users to make informed decisions, fostering confidence in navigating digital financial platforms while reducing the risks of falling victim to scams.
